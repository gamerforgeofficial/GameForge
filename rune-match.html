NEON RIFT

Slip through the rift, collect cyan energy orbs and avoid the roaming sentinels. Survive for 60 seconds to win.

Keyboard: WASD / Arrow keys · Space = dash · P = pause

Movement
Use WASD or arrow keys. On mobile, tap the directional buttons.
Rift dash
Press Space or DASH to burst forward. Use it to escape danger; it recharges quickly.
