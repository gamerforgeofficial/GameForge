Quick rules
1. Roll the dice on your turn.
2. You need a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
3. Move a token by the number shown on the dice.
4. Landing on an unprotected opponent token sends it back to base.
5. Get all four of your tokens home first to win.

Online: sign in, create a room or join using a room code. The host starts once at least two players have joined.

Choose a mode to begin — the game will not start automatically.
